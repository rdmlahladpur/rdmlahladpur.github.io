Welcome to Government Degree College, Lahladpur

राजकीय डिग्री महाविद्यालय, लहलादपुर में आपका स्वागत है

The college is the first government degree college of Lahladpur block in Saran district. It was opened in July 2026 as one of 211 new government degree colleges set up by the Government of Bihar in blocks that had no degree college of their own.

As a constituent unit of Jai Prakash University, Chapra, the college teaches the Bachelor of Arts programme in six subjects, so that the young people of Lahladpur can complete their graduation close to home.

    About the College महाविद्यालय परिचय

    Government Degree College, Lahladpur, officially Rajkiya Degree Mahavidyalaya, Lahladpur, was opened on 15 July 2026. On that day the Government of Bihar started classes in 211 new government degree colleges across the state, one in each block that did not have a degree college before.

    Most of Bihar's older colleges are in district towns, and many students from villages, especially girls, could not travel that far every day. A college in the block itself removes that barrier.

    The college is a constituent unit of Jai Prakash University, Chapra. The university conducts admissions and examinations and awards the degree. The college runs the classes, guides students and keeps their records.

    Vision

    To make good higher education available to every young person of Lahladpur block.

    Mission

    • To teach every student with care, in a language they understand.
    • To encourage daughters of the area to complete their graduation.
    • To prepare students for further study, competitive examinations and work.

    Principal's Message प्राचार्य का संदेश

    Dear students and parents,

    Until this year, a young person in Lahladpur who wanted a degree had to travel far for it. Many could not, and our daughters were usually the first to give up. This college has been opened so that distance no longer decides who gets to study.

    We teach six subjects in the Bachelor of Arts programme. Our teachers are qualified, and they are here for every student who comes through our gate, whatever school or village that student comes from.

    I request parents to send their children to college, and I request students to attend classes regularly, ask questions freely and make full use of what the college offers. A degree earned with regular study opens the door to further education, competitive examinations and good work.

    Our doors are open. You are welcome to visit the college.

    Dr. Ramanuj SinghFounder Principal, Government Degree College, Lahladpur

    Admission नामांकन

    Admission to the college is done through the online admission process of Jai Prakash University, Chapra.

    Eligibility: students who have passed Intermediate (10+2) from a recognised board can apply. Seats and reservation follow the rules of Jai Prakash University and the Government of Bihar.

    How to take admission

    1. Check the admission noticeThe university publishes the admission schedule on jpv.ac.in.
    2. Apply onlineFill the form on the JPU admission portal, jpvadm.samarth.edu.in. Choose Government Degree College, Lahladpur and your subject.
    3. Check the merit listThe university releases merit lists on the portal. Note your rank and the reporting dates.
    4. Report to the collegeBring your original documents for verification and complete the admission by paying the fee.

    Documents required

    • Matric and Intermediate marksheets and certificates
    • School or College Leaving Certificate
    • Migration certificate, if you passed from a board outside Bihar
    • Caste, residential and income certificates, if claiming reservation
    • Aadhaar card
    • Recent passport-size photographs
